🎤 Talk with your friends?

Dark Hunt has proximity voice chat: you hear players louder when they're close to you and not at all when they're far away.

Your browser will ask for permission to use the microphone. Voice is sent directly between players and is never recorded.

🎧 Please use headphones to avoid echo.

(Without a mic you can still play and hear everyone nearby - you just can't talk.)
